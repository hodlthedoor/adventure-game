# World and puzzle map

All ordinary links are reciprocal. Main routes, with directions read left to right:

- Village square N well house D well shaft D arrival hall.
- Hall W sluice landing W pump room N valve gallery E cistern rim.
- Hall N garden gate N mushroom grove E keeper's hut N nursery.
- Hall E cloakroom E inventory office N storeroom E bell loft.
- Hall S processional passage S celebration chamber E overflow balcony.

Two shortcuts: grove W cistern rim, opened from the grove; nursery E storeroom, opened from the nursery. The garden's inner gate needs irrigation; the procession needs bell and living light; the balcony opens after victory.

The fixed retreat direction in each dark room follows its main route backwards to the hall. Every retreat remains available without tools. The hall, shaft, and surface are naturally lit.

## Main puzzle dependencies

1. Brace the pump with the landing's wedge (or tuning fork), then start it.
2. Use the diversion valve after the pump is stable to restore water.
3. Use irrigation at the garden gate after restoring water.
4. Read/carry the office manifest and use it on the cabinet for cup and bell. This branch is independent of the waterworks.
5. Offer the cup (or miniature crown) to the keeper for living light.
6. Install living light in the hall pedestal, then ring the bell for the procession.
7. Open the ceremony vent, admit water with the rainwheel, then ring the bell.

The bell-loft inscription explains the final sequence. Pump and flood hazards allow three spent actions after activation; examination, hints, and help are free. Escape east from the pump, or north twice from the ceremony to reset its pressure.

## Treasures

- Tuning fork: cistern rim; can replace the wedge, and vice versa.
- Glass seed: use the watered grove's seedpod.
- Moon pearl: use living light on nursery buds.
- Miniature crown: use the manifest on the storeroom drawer; may serve as the keeper's cup, then be exchanged back.
- Rain crystal: balcony after victory.

Treasures count when carried or left at the well house. After victory, exploration and safe optional interactions remain available without fuel or hazards, so missing treasures can still be recovered.
