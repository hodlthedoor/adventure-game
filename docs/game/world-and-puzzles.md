# World and puzzle map

All ordinary links are reciprocal. Main routes, with directions read left to right:

- Village square N well house D well shaft D arrival hall.
- Hall W sluice landing W pump room N valve gallery E cistern rim.
- Hall N garden gate N mushroom grove E keeper's hut N nursery.
- Hall E cloakroom E inventory office N storeroom E bell loft.
- Hall S processional passage S celebration chamber E overflow balcony.

Two shortcuts: grove W cistern rim, opened from the grove; nursery E storeroom, opened from the nursery. The garden's inner gate needs irrigation; the procession needs bell and living light; the balcony opens after victory.

The fixed retreat direction in each dark room follows its main route backwards to the hall. Every retreat remains available without tools. The hall, shaft, and surface are naturally lit.
